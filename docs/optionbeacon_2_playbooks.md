# OptionBeacon 2.0 Playbook Catalog

## Framework

A playbook is a versioned hypothesis about a particular market behavior. It
declares its prerequisites, setup formation, confirmation, plan policy,
anti-conditions, and research gate. Every playbook starts disabled.

Common output fields are direction, formation state, confirmation state, entry
zone, maximum entry, invalidation, structural stop, targets, expected duration,
required evidence, conflicts, missing data, and definition version.

Minimum activation samples below are initial research governance targets, not
claims of statistical sufficiency. Samples must be completed, eligible,
point-in-time trades with adequate regime coverage and a held-out evaluation
cohort.

## 1. Trend Continuation

- **Ideal market:** Directional SPY/QQQ regime with aligned symbol and sector.
- **Prerequisites:** Established higher-timeframe trend, orderly intraday
  structure, acceptable volatility and risk/reward.
- **Formation:** Impulse, controlled pause/pullback, preservation of the prior
  structural pivot.
- **Confirmation:** Break from consolidation or resumption through a defined
  pivot with participation.
- **Entry:** Retest or narrow confirmation zone; never unbounded breakout chase.
- **Stop:** Beyond the pullback pivot or thesis-defining structure.
- **Targets:** Prior extension, measured move, then next structural level.
- **Invalidation:** Trend structure breaks or broad context reverses before
  entry.
- **Expected duration:** 30-240 minutes.
- **Avoid:** Choppy regime, climactic extension, low participation, imminent
  event.
- **Research:** Segment by regime, pullback depth, time of day, and extension.
- **Minimum sample:** 100 total, at least 30 per direction and 25 held out.

## 2. Opening Range Breakout

- **Ideal market:** Directional open, meaningful overnight catalyst/gap, and
  expanding participation.
- **Prerequisites:** Defined opening-range window, fresh bars, acceptable range
  width, aligned market/relative strength.
- **Formation:** Price tests a range boundary without invalidating the opposite
  side.
- **Confirmation:** Close through the boundary plus hold/retest or explicit
  volume policy.
- **Entry:** Confirmed boundary/retest zone.
- **Stop:** Back inside the range beyond the failed-break threshold.
- **Targets:** Range projection and next higher-timeframe level.
- **Invalidation:** Failed hold, opposite range break, or excessive extension.
- **Expected duration:** 15-180 minutes.
- **Avoid:** Abnormally wide range, low volume, conflicting benchmark, late-day
  first break.
- **Research:** Opening-window length, gap type, retest requirement, ambiguous
  bars.
- **Minimum sample:** 150 total across volatility regimes, 40 held out.

## 3. Opening Range Failure

- **Ideal market:** Failed directional open in mixed/reversing context.
- **Prerequisites:** Genuine opening-range break followed by rejection and
  room toward the opposite side.
- **Formation:** Price returns inside the range after breakout.
- **Confirmation:** Failure pivot breaks in the reversal direction and the
  rejected boundary holds.
- **Entry:** Inside-range retest after failure confirmation.
- **Stop:** Beyond the failed-break extreme.
- **Targets:** Range midpoint, opposite boundary, then next external level.
- **Invalidation:** Price reclaims and holds outside the failed boundary.
- **Expected duration:** 20-180 minutes.
- **Avoid:** Strong trend/participation supporting the original break.
- **Research:** Failure depth, time-to-fail, gap direction, benchmark agreement.
- **Minimum sample:** 120 total, 30 per direction and 30 held out.

## 4. VWAP Reclaim

- **Ideal market:** Bullish recovery or bearish-covering context after price
  trades below session VWAP.
- **Prerequisites:** Valid session VWAP, identifiable support, improving
  relative strength, sufficient reward.
- **Formation:** Repeated VWAP test or compression below VWAP.
- **Confirmation:** Close above VWAP followed by hold/retest; direction is
  bullish.
- **Entry:** VWAP hold/retest zone with capped maximum entry.
- **Stop:** Below reclaim pivot or structural support.
- **Targets:** Intraday swing, opening-range/prior-day level, extension.
- **Invalidation:** Acceptance back below VWAP.
- **Expected duration:** 20-180 minutes.
- **Avoid:** Strong bearish regime, falling participation, very late reclaim.
- **Research:** VWAP calculation consistency, reclaim duration, regime.
- **Minimum sample:** 120 total and 30 held out.

## 5. VWAP Rejection

- **Ideal market:** Bearish continuation or failed bullish recovery beneath
  VWAP.
- **Prerequisites:** Valid VWAP, bearish structure, room to support, acceptable
  risk.
- **Formation:** Rally into VWAP from below.
- **Confirmation:** Rejection and break of the pullback pivot; direction is
  bearish.
- **Entry:** Rejection/retest zone below VWAP.
- **Stop:** Above rejection high and invalidation buffer.
- **Targets:** Intraday low, prior/overnight low, extension.
- **Invalidation:** Sustained reclaim above VWAP.
- **Expected duration:** 20-180 minutes.
- **Avoid:** Strong bullish regime or capitulation far below support.
- **Research:** Symmetry with reclaim, regime, rejection count, time of day.
- **Minimum sample:** 120 total and 30 held out.

## 6. Pullback Continuation

- **Ideal market:** Mature but non-exhausted trend with orderly retracement.
- **Prerequisites:** Prior impulse, intact swing structure, pullback into a
  predeclared level, participation cools rather than collapses.
- **Formation:** Countertrend retracement with reduced momentum.
- **Confirmation:** Directional pivot reclaim/break and renewed participation.
- **Entry:** Confirmation pivot or first controlled retest.
- **Stop:** Beyond pullback extreme.
- **Targets:** Prior impulse extreme and measured extension.
- **Invalidation:** Pullback becomes structural reversal.
- **Expected duration:** 30-240 minutes.
- **Avoid:** Deep chaotic pullback, event shock, no room to prior extreme.
- **Research:** Pullback depth/duration and distinction from Trend Continuation.
- **Minimum sample:** 150 total, 40 held out.

## 7. Compression Expansion

- **Ideal market:** Contracting range/volatility near a meaningful level with a
  plausible catalyst or directional context.
- **Prerequisites:** Quantified compression, sufficient liquidity, defined
  boundaries, acceptable post-break risk.
- **Formation:** Range and volatility contract over a fixed window.
- **Confirmation:** Boundary break plus range/volume expansion and hold.
- **Entry:** Boundary/retest zone.
- **Stop:** Inside compression beyond failure threshold.
- **Targets:** Compression-height projections and structural levels.
- **Invalidation:** Return through the range or no expansion within expiry.
- **Expected duration:** 15-180 minutes.
- **Avoid:** Random low-liquidity compression or breakout directly into a level.
- **Research:** Compression definitions, false breaks, expansion confirmation.
- **Minimum sample:** 150 total, 40 held out.

## 8. Gap Continuation

- **Ideal market:** Meaningful premarket gap with catalyst/context and early
  acceptance in the gap direction.
- **Prerequisites:** Reliable prior close and extended-hours data, gap
  classification, liquidity, aligned participation.
- **Formation:** Initial balance holds without filling material gap.
- **Confirmation:** Break/hold in gap direction after opening volatility.
- **Entry:** Confirmed range boundary or pullback.
- **Stop:** Beneath/above opening structure or gap acceptance level.
- **Targets:** Premarket level, measured range, higher-timeframe level.
- **Invalidation:** Material gap fill or loss of acceptance.
- **Expected duration:** 20-240 minutes.
- **Avoid:** Unexplained thin gaps, imminent catalyst, extreme opening range.
- **Research:** Requires reliable extended-hours/catalyst data.
- **Minimum sample:** 150 classified gaps, 40 held out.

## 9. Gap Reversal

- **Ideal market:** Exhausted or unsupported gap that fails to hold.
- **Prerequisites:** Reliable gap data, rejection structure, sufficient room
  toward prior close.
- **Formation:** Gap extension fails and price returns through an acceptance
  level.
- **Confirmation:** Reversal pivot plus relative-strength reversal.
- **Entry:** Failure/retest zone toward gap fill.
- **Stop:** Beyond gap-session extreme.
- **Targets:** Opening level, partial gap fill, prior close.
- **Invalidation:** Reacceptance in the original gap direction.
- **Expected duration:** 30-240 minutes.
- **Avoid:** Strong catalyst continuation and persistent broad alignment.
- **Research:** Catalyst categories, gap size, volume, and partial/full fills.
- **Minimum sample:** 150 classified gaps, 40 held out.

## 10. Support/Resistance Reversal

- **Ideal market:** Price reaches a predeclared higher-timeframe level in a
  balanced or exhausting regime.
- **Prerequisites:** Level existed before the test, rejection evidence,
  bounded stop, and sufficient target distance.
- **Formation:** Test/sweep of level without acceptance beyond it.
- **Confirmation:** Reversal pivot and momentum/participation shift.
- **Entry:** Reclaim/rejection zone after confirmation.
- **Stop:** Beyond sweep extreme.
- **Targets:** VWAP, range midpoint, opposite structure.
- **Invalidation:** Acceptance beyond the level.
- **Expected duration:** 20-240 minutes.
- **Avoid:** First touch during powerful trend expansion without failure.
- **Research:** Objective level hierarchy and multiple-test effects.
- **Minimum sample:** 150 total, 40 held out.

## 11. Relative Strength Divergence

- **Ideal market:** Benchmark pullback/rally while the symbol persistently
  outperforms/underperforms near actionable structure.
- **Prerequisites:** Synchronized symbol/benchmark bars, stable relative metric,
  symbol-specific structure.
- **Formation:** Relative line diverges while absolute price holds a level.
- **Confirmation:** Absolute price breaks in the relative-strength direction.
- **Entry:** Structure confirmation zone.
- **Stop:** Beyond the absolute-price thesis level.
- **Targets:** Prior swing and relative-strength projected level.
- **Invalidation:** Relative divergence disappears and structure breaks.
- **Expected duration:** 20-180 minutes.
- **Avoid:** Benchmark data gaps or purely beta-driven moves.
- **Research:** Benchmark choice, synchronization, sector adjustment.
- **Minimum sample:** 150 total, 40 held out.

## Overlap and selection

Several playbooks may observe one symbol. The engine must not blend them into a
synthetic universal setup. It should:

1. evaluate each independently;
2. reject unmet prerequisites;
3. record overlapping candidates;
4. rank eligible playbooks by evidence coherence, plan quality, research
   maturity, and data completeness;
5. expose conflicts when two eligible playbooks imply opposite directions;
6. select at most one actionable playbook per opportunity identity.

Activation requires a versioned registry change, replay report, held-out
results, shadow stability, paper validation, rollback plan, and approval.
